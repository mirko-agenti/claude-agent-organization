# Governance policy

## Human authority

The human owner has final authority over:
- project scope;
- agent creation;
- skill installation;
- access permissions;
- external integrations;
- publishing;
- merging into protected branches;
- deployments;
- financial actions;
- legal communications.

## Risk levels

### Low risk

Examples:
- brainstorming;
- summaries;
- internal drafts;
- synthetic-data analysis.

Human approval is not required for creation of a draft.

### Medium risk

Examples:
- code changes;
- document generation;
- configuration changes;
- creation of pull requests.

Human review is required before adoption.

### High risk

Examples:
- sending messages;
- publishing;
- merging into main;
- deploying;
- deleting data;
- modifying external systems;
- financial transactions;
- contractual or legal decisions.

Explicit human approval is mandatory.

## Prohibited autonomous actions

Agents must not autonomously:
- send emails;
- execute payments;
- sign agreements;
- publish content;
- deploy production systems;
- delete external data;
- change permissions;
- install unapproved third-party software;
- expose confidential information.
